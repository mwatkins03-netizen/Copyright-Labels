Make your label. Paste it everywhere.

Two minutes. The label tells students to use the campus AI (Copilot or Gemini with go.olemiss.edu) and tells any other AI to stop. The same block of text works in a syllabus, an assignment page, a PDF footer, slide notes and email.

Give students the prompt too. "Copy the student prompt" is a message students paste into any AI before any course material. In testing it stopped every model; the label alone stopped about half. The whole routine · the odds.
Not foolproof. Not meant to be.

The label is a request with a copyright line behind it. It cannot stop an upload. In testing across six AI model configurations, about half honored an embedded label every time, and all of them stopped when the student sent the prompt first. The real protection is the campus tool; the label and the prompt point to it. Full results.
