Campus Only Label

A label for assignments that says, to students and to AI systems alike, whether your copyrighted course material may be processed by any AI, by the campus-licensed AI only, or by none.

    B4 · Export

    Put it on the assignment

    Every export carries the badge, the student sentence and a short notice to AI systems; the full terms sit behind the label link. Nothing is stored anywhere; the label lives in the link. See this label on a syllabus, an assignment, a slide and a handout →

    Not foolproof. Not meant to be.

    The label is a request with a copyright line behind it. It cannot stop an upload, and no AI company is obliged to honor it. Here is what it did in testing on 2026-11-04 (agent harnesses, not the consumer apps; see the results):

    The protection comes from three things together: the label for the record, the ask-your-AI-first prompt for the moment of use, and the campus tool for the actual contract.

    For students: ask your AI first. The prompt below contains only the label. A student pastes it as their own message before sharing any course content, and the assistant checks the label and replies STOP or PROCEED. In testing, an explicit check like this held in every GPT run and is the one move that reliably changes Sonnet's behavior. A model's reply is still not proof of campus coverage.

    About this label
    
      
    Campus Only Label COL/1.0 is a request, not a lock: it tells people and machines what the copyright holder permits, in words a chatbot can act on. It does not technically prevent an upload. Why it matters, and what to do about the rest.
