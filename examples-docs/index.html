Download, open, read the yellow box

All seven use the same UM course label (Campus AI only, Copilot or Gemini with go.olemiss.edu) except the exam, which uses the No AI processing label. Every file carries the label in three layers: the badge for people, the two sentences for the record, the small grey notice for AI systems.

FileDocument typeWhere the label goes
01-syllabus-with-label.docxSyllabus (Word)Once, under the AI policy heading, at whole-course scope. One-line version in the page footer.
02-assignment-sheet-with-label.docxAssignment sheet (Word)After the directions, before the submission instructions. One-line version in the footer.
03-exam-with-label.docxExam or student work (Word)A separate No AI processing label: top of page one and the footer of every page.
04-reading-handout-with-label.pdfReading handout (PDF)Footer of every page in small type; the full block once on page one.
05-slides-with-label.pptxSlide deck (PowerPoint)Badge in the footer of the title and last slides; the label as text on the last slide; the notice in the speaker notes.
06-lms-assignment-page.htmlLMS assignment page (HTML)Paste the HTML export after the directions and before the submission box; check that the grey text survived saving.
07-email-announcement.txtEmail or announcement (plain text)Two sentences and the links in the body; the notice under a divider at the end.
The rule behind all seven. Label the course once on the syllabus. Put the one-line version in the footer of everything else. Make a separate No-AI label only for exams and anything containing student work. Give students the ask-your-AI-first link; it is in every label.
