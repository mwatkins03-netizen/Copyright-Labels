X1 · Syllabus

On the syllabus

Paste the inline HTML (export E3) into the syllabus, or the plain text (E4) into Word. Put it once, near the AI-use policy, with scope set to the whole course. Students see the badge and two sentences; the short notice sits under it in small grey type, and the full terms are behind the label link.

X2 · Assignment

In assignment directions (Blackboard, Canvas)

The paste-in HTML survives the LMS editor. The notice to AI systems rides along in the collapsed block, so a student who copies the whole page into a chatbot copies the notice too.

X3 · Slide

On a slide

The badge image (E5) in the footer of the title slide and the last slide, plus one spoken sentence. An image is the one layer a model cannot reliably read, so the slide also carries the student line as text.

X4 · Handout

In a handout or PDF footer

The plain text (E4) in the page footer of every page. When a student photographs or uploads a page, the notice is in the frame.

Open the handout print view ↗
X5 · Email

In an announcement or email

The student line, the link, and the notice under a divider. Mail clients strip HTML unpredictably; plain text does not break.

X6 · Website

On a course website

The head tags on every page and the robots.txt at the root (E6). These speak to crawlers before a page is ever opened.
